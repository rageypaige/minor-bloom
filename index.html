on a phone?

Phones don’t let websites hear other apps, so Minor Bloom can’t listen to Spotify playing on this phone.

Instead, play your song on another speaker or computer and choose microphone, or pick a song saved on your phone with audio file.

using spotify?

In Chrome or Edge, play your song at open.spotify.com in another tab. Click listen to a tab, choose that tab and turn on Share tab audio. Keep this window visible while you listen.

On Windows you can share your entire screen with Share system audio on instead, which works with the Spotify app.
