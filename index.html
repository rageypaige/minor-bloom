Play a song and watch it paint itself.

How to listen to Spotify
  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.
  2. Open your song in the Spotify web player (open.spotify.com) in another tab and press play.
  3. Back here, click Listen to a tab or my computer, choose the Spotify tab and switch on Share tab audio.
  4. On Windows you can also share your entire screen with Share system audio on, which works with the Spotify app.
  5. Keep this window visible while it paints. Browsers pause pages hidden in background tabs.
